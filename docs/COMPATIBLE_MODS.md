# Mods that work well with Roadside Emergencies

None of these are required. The one thing Roadside Emergencies does require is the free Emergency Pack DLC. Only what is under "Tested" has been played with this mod.

## Tested

| Mod | Author | What was tested | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|---|
| T880 Rollback | Roughneck Modding Crew | A rolled-over tractor pulled onto the bed with our strap and the reel keys, belted down, driven in. A wrecked truck pulled onto the bed from an angle with our strap | [itch.io](https://roughneckmoddingcrew.itch.io/t880-rollback), [KingMods](https://www.kingmods.net/en/fs25/mods/63989/kenworth-t880-rollback) |
| International CV Rollback | Roughneck Modding Crew | A full wrecked truck job: loaded, driven to the recovery point and paid | [itch.io](https://roughneckmoddingcrew.itch.io/fs25-cv-rollback) |
| Better Winch | ThundR | Its winch hooks a dead animal, with a small gap because the body is a little larger than the model. The T880's own winch hooks a wrecked truck and the bale trailer on the attach points we offer, and pulls them onto the bed when the rollback is lined up with them | [itch.io](https://thundrfs.itch.io/fs25-better-winch) |
| UniversalAutoload | loki_79 | Loads dead animals onto trailers it supports | ModHub |

The base game's front loaders, bale grabs, forks, tension belts and crane trailers also handle dead animals, because the bodies work like bales. A small crane can't lift a cow, which weighs about 650 kg.

## How the winches fit in

Better Winch, and every RMC truck that uses it, hooks a vehicle only where a trailer would hitch up. A tractor or a truck has no such place, so the winch refuses it. A wreck standing on its wheels offers Better Winch two attach points of its own, one on each end, through that mod's own function for this. Without Better Winch nothing changes.

A wreck on its side still has to be righted with our strap, because it is held until our strap goes tight.

Better Winch uses the game's own physics rope, so it pulls along the line, but it doesn't steer what it pulls (its scripts have no steering code), and our short hitch for towing is only on our strap. A wreck on its winch rolls where its wheels point. Our strap turns the wreck's front wheels toward the pull. That is the reason not to require Better Winch: it wouldn't line a wreck up any better, and it would make every player fetch another mod.
