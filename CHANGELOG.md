# Changelog

Versions match the mod's version in the game.

## 0.9.9.4

- **The pager beeps** when a call comes in, at the game's interface volume. The settings (`H`) can turn it off.

## 0.9.9.3: first public beta

**Requires the free Emergency Pack DLC.** Everything is new.

- **19 new callouts for the free Emergency Pack:** eight kinds of animal hit on the road, a wrecked truck, a tractor rollover, an overturned trailer with its bales, a livestock trailer crash with loose animals to catch, storm damage after a twister, and a downed power line.
- **A railroad crossing:** a stalled vehicle, the map's own train on its way, and a clock. Only a strap or a winch line moves it, and you can fail.
- **A car in the water:** the driver still inside, and a strap to pull it up the bank. Only on maps with shallow water and a gentle bank beside a road.
- **A tree on a car:** the driver on the horn inside, and a log to get off the car before the door will open.
- **A flock on the road:** drive or walk the sheep into the farmer's pen. The sheep get out on roads through open country, and the pen stands in a meadow nearby, never in a town, a yard, the woods or by the railroad. They start in bunches along the road and move straight away from you, so you go round them to steer them. Each honk brings a bunch a little closer together, and they scatter if you come in too fast or lean on the horn.
- **You only get calls you can finish.** Dispatch checks what your farm owns before every call: if you don't have what a job takes, that call doesn't come.
- **Towing with any vehicle:** once you drive off, the strap closes up to a short hitch and the wreck follows like a trailer, from either end.
- **A logging crash:** real logs across the road, for log forks, cranes, straps and chainsaws. The trailer stays until the owner's crew has been.
- **The pager tells you everything before you answer,** a line each: where the call is and which way, what is there (the animals, or the vehicle and its load), what the job takes, which of your vehicles can do it, how long you have, and about what it pays. What it says is what you find.
- **A job you said yes to and that ends with no pay costs a small charge:** a tenth of its pay. Saying no costs nothing.
- **Different wrecks:** trucks, tractors, trailers, livestock trailers and cars from the base game change from call to call, and the bigger ones come up less often.
- **You choose how often calls come,** and every kind of call is as likely as any other.
- **Calls come to you** from anywhere on the map's roads, and traffic stops behind them until the road is clear.
- **Use what you own:** dead animals work like bales, so anything that handles bales handles them, and the fill display counts them ("3 Deer"). The recovery strap hooks where you aim, and reels a wreck onto a rollback.
- **Pay for doing it right:** drop-off and recovery points, your own points, warning-light and two-part bonuses, and a county bill for what you leave behind.
- **Scene kit:** cones, barricades and taped cones, free, placed by hand, and they stop traffic.
- **In-game guide and settings** on `H`: 21 pages on everything you need to know, from what each call takes to how the pay works. A welcome the first time you play, with where to report a bug.
- **Your currency and units:** money, distances and weights as the game writes them, in dollars, euros or pounds, feet or metres.
- **A call panel** at the right of the screen while a job runs: what it is, how far and which way, the clock that counts, every step of the job ticked off as you do it, the warning lights bonus and about what it pays. It can be turned off in the settings.
- **The county crew drives up:** at a downed power line, a crew truck comes along the road from out of sight with its hazards on, parks short of the scene, and the crew cuts the power. While you stand too close to the live wires, a red warning with a hazard sign says so. The poles only stand on open ground beside the road, never on farmland or in or against a building; where there is no room, the call comes from another road. Bringing the pole in is optional and pays extra.
- **The warning lights bonus** needs lights the vehicle really has, kept on at the scene for 20 seconds.
- **A new savegame starts with your last settings.**
- **Every warning starts with "WARNING"**, as well as the game's orange.
- **Every line of ours in the F1 help starts with "RSE:"** and names the item in your hands; texts name your own keys, also after you change them.
- **Nothing is left lying about:** however a job ends, done, handed over or failed, what is left at the scene stays until you have gone, then the county clears it away, also across a save. Your cones and barricades, and dead animals you loaded, stay yours.
- **Logs stay where you left them:** the logs of a logging crash, a tree on a car and a downed power line come back where they were saved. Other logs are left to the game.

**Do I need to do anything?** Copy your savegame first: this is a beta. Saving during a call is fine.
