# Changelog

Versions match the mod's version in the game.

## 0.9.0.0: first public beta

Everything is new.

- **14 new callouts for the free Emergency Pack:** eight kinds of animal hit on the road, a wrecked truck, a tractor rollover, an overturned trailer with its bales, a livestock trailer crash with loose animals to catch, storm damage after a twister, and a downed power line.
- **Calls come to you** from anywhere on the map's roads, and traffic stops behind them until the road is clear.
- **Use what you own:** dead animals work like bales, so anything that handles bales handles them. The recovery strap hooks where you aim, and reels a wreck onto a rollback.
- **Pay for doing it right:** drop-off and recovery points, your own points, warning-light and two-part bonuses, and a county bill for what you leave behind.
- **Scene kit:** cones, barricades and taped cones, free, placed by hand, and they stop traffic.
- **In-game guide and settings** on `H`.

**Do I need to do anything?** Copy your savegame first: this is a beta. Saving during a call is fine.
